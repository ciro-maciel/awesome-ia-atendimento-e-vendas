## Privacy in AI

Where to turn off the use of your conversations to train the model.

- [Anthropic: onde mudar o treino](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-anthropic-onde-mudar-o-treino) - How to stop your conversations in Claude from being used to train the model. `official`
- [Google: atividade do Gemini](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-google-atividade-do-gemini) - How to turn off Gemini's activity history and what changes with it. `official`
- [Microsoft: controles de privacidade do Copilot](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-microsoft-controles-de-privacidad) - Where to find the privacy and training controls for Copilot. `official`
- [OpenAI: controles de dados](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-openai-controles-de-dados) - How to stop conversations in ChatGPT from being used to train the model. `official`
- [WhatsApp: privacidade](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-whatsapp-privacidade) - What WhatsApp says about privacy, including when you talk to Meta AI. `official`

## Consumer service rules

What the customer has the right to demand.

- [Código de Defesa do Consumidor](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-codigo-de-defesa-do-consumidor) - The CDC compiled on Planalto. `official` `free`
- [Decreto do comércio eletrônico](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-decreto-do-comercio-eletronico) - Decree 7.962/2013, with the rules for those who sell online. `official` `free`
- [Decreto do SAC](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-decreto-do-sac) - Decree 11.034/2022, with the rules for consumer service. `official` `free`
- [Lei do atendimento prioritário](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-lei-do-atendimento-prioritario) - Law 10.048/2000, on who has priority in service. `official` `free`
- [Não Me Perturbe](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-nao-me-perturbe) - The registry to block telemarketing calls from carriers and banks. `free`
- [Prefixo 0303 na Anatel](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-prefixo-0303-na-anatel) - The rule requiring outbound telemarketing to call with the 0303 prefix. `official` `free`

## Rights of service workers

The labor rules for retail and telemarketing.

- [Lei do comerciário](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-lei-do-comerciario) - Law 12.790/2013, which regulates the retail worker profession. `official` `free`
- [Lei do vendedor](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-lei-do-vendedor) - Law 3.207/1957, on salespeople, travelers and pracistas. `official` `free`
- [TST: cobrança de metas por WhatsApp](https://go.mymarketing.click/iavend-tst-cobranca-de-metas-por-whatsap) - The TST decision on charging sales targets outside working hours. `official` `free`

## Data protection

The law and the authority that apply to customer and student data.

## Labels

Each item says what it is. The labels next to it help you choose: official when it comes from the body that sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid based on the usage model, API and SDK based on how you integrate. Items tagged "by author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
